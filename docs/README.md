## 🚀 Key Features

- **Personal Todo Lists** - Every member gets their own task management system
- **Group Collaboration** - Assign tasks to group members
- **Smart Reminders** - Email and BuddyPress notifications for due dates
- **Custom Categories** - Organize tasks your way
- **Priority Levels** - Mark tasks as high, medium, or low priority
- **Progress Tracking** - Monitor completion rates
- **Shortcode Integration** - Display todos anywhere on your site
- **Mobile Responsive** - Works perfectly on all devices
