# What's new in 4.0.1

Version 4.0.1 fixes group task completion, adds language-pack translations and tightens accessibility.

## Group tasks

- Group members see the tasks assigned to them on the Todos tab and can complete them. See [Group to-dos](../features/group-todos).
- The tab count and stats match what each viewer sees.
- The assign picker lists the first 100 members A to Z. The `bptodo_assignee_picker_limit` filter changes the number.
- Group and notification features load only when the BuddyPress Groups or Notifications component is active.
- Members assigned a group task get their on-site notification again. On BuddyBoss, group todo notifications and emails open the group's todo tab.
- Deleting your copy of a group task deletes only your copy. Editing a group task updates every member's copy, and each member keeps their own completed state.
- The unlinked Reports page and its table library are removed. The Overview sub-tab covers per-member progress.

## Accessibility and appearance

- Each checkbox is announced with its task title, and touch targets are 40px.
- Edit moves focus to the title field, and Delete moves focus to the next task.
- Toast messages are announced politely.
- Edit and Delete are fully visible, and dark mode is easier to read.

## Other

- Translations load from WordPress.org language packs. The text domain is now `bp-user-to-do-list`, so rename your own translation files from `wb-todo-{locale}` to `bp-user-to-do-list-{locale}`.
- Automatic cleanup measures task age in UTC and keeps member copies of group tasks that do not match the chosen status. See [Data Retention](../settings/admin-settings#data-retention-auto-cleanup).
- Changing a task's due date sends its reminder again for the new date.
- Group moderator settings are enforced for every way of adding or changing a group task, and stats for another member are returned only to site administrators.
- Due dates follow the site's timezone.
